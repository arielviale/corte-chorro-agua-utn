# Project Rules & Design System Guidelines: Waterjet Cutting Presentation (AWJ 4.0)

## Context & Objectives
- **Topic:** Academic & Technical Presentation on Abrasive Waterjet Cutting (AWJ 4.0 Cyber-Physical / Mechatronics UTN FRP).
- **Goal:** Replace the overcrowded "dark cyberpunk/neon dashboard" template with a high-impact, professional, and visually engaging slide presentation structure.
- **Medium:** Projected presentation slides (clean, high-contrast, instantly readable from a distance).

---

## 1. UI & Visual Aesthetics Rules (Non-Negotiable)

### Color Palette & Tone
- **Forbidden:** Cyberpunk neon glows, cyan/teal saturation, cluttered topbar status badges, and generic dark dashboard themes.
- **Primary Aesthetic:** **Clean Industrial Swiss / Technical Precision.**
- **Default Canvas:** **Light Mode / White Backgrounds** for all content slides (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA`).
- **Dark Canvas Limit:** Maximum **2 dark slides** across the entire presentation (Title/Opener Slide and Closing/Q&A Slide ONLY).
- **Color Roles:**
  - **Ground (60%):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Soft Off-White (`#F4F5F7`).
  - **Accent (30%):** Deep Slate / Industrial Charcoal (`#0F172A` or `#1E293B`).
  - **Pop / Metric Highlight (10%):** High-Precision Orange (`#FF5722` or `#EA580C`) to indicate key metrics, physical values, and critical takeaways.

### Typography
- **Primary Sans-Serif:** Clean, highly legible sans-serif (`Inter`, `Space Grotesk`, or `DM Sans`) for all headings and body copy.
- **Monospace Restriction:** Reserved **ONLY** for exact numeric data, physical units, or CNC formulas (e.g., `P_UHP: 6200 bar`, `V_CHORRO: 1.050 m/s`). NEVER use monospace for titles or narrative paragraphs.
- **Font Sizes:**
  - Headings: ≥ 32px
  - Body & Bullet Text: ≥ 18px (No dense text paragraphs).

---

## 2. Layout & Presentation Density Rules

### Copywriting Grammar & Density
- **No Paragraph Soup:** Convert prose into **Headline / Fragment Grammar** (e.g., `Bold Keyword: Fragment`).
- **Card Content Limit:** Card bodies must contain 1 single concise phrase (3–8 words max).
- **Word Limit:** Target ~40 words per slide total.
- **Negative Space:** Maintain at least 50% whitespace on every slide.

### Layout Variety
- Alternate layouts across slides: Big Metric Callouts, 2–3 Column Feature Cards, Side-by-Side Graphic & Metric splits, and Horizontal Process Timelines.
- **Never repeat** the same layout structure on consecutive slides.

---

## 3. Waterjet Cutting Key Content Framework

Ensure technical accuracy and prominence for the following core technical metrics:
- **Zero Heat-Affected Zone:** `ZAC = 0,00 mm` (Cold Cut / Corte en Frío).
- **Ultra-High Pressure (UHP):** Up to `6.480 bar` / `94.000 PSI` (Flow HyperJet).
- **Supersonic Jet Velocity:** `V_CHORRO ≈ 1.050 m/s (Mach 3.06)`.
- **Active Abrasive Flow:** Barton Garnet Mesh 80 (~1,1 M particles/s).
- **Multi-Axis Kinematics:** Dynamic XD / 5-Axis Tilt-A-Jet (`θ = 0,00°` Taper Control).
